# Validation cohorts and test-report counts

These records describe separate historical executions. Their original source hashes, outcomes and bytes remain unchanged. A count of pytest call reports is not a count of distinct test methods: an enclosing method can emit a passed `TestReport` even when its child `SubtestReport` records fail.

| Metadata cohort | Distinct test nodeids | TestReport call records | SubtestReport call records | Outcome |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| [a3](evidence/run_2026-10-28/validation/integration_2026-10-28/SOURCE_REVIEW_METADATA_CHECK.a3.json) | 110 | 110 passed | 150 passed | Passed; 260 call records, 480 total phase records |
| [a4 red](evidence/run_2026-10-28/validation/integration_2026-10-28/SOURCE_REVIEW_METADATA_CHECK.a4.red.json) | 120 | 118 passed, 2 failed | 166 passed, 4 failed | Expected failed regression run; 284 passed of 290 call records, 530 total phase records |
| [a4 green](evidence/run_2026-10-28/validation/integration_2026-10-28/SOURCE_REVIEW_METADATA_CHECK.a4.green.json) | 120 | 120 passed | 170 passed | Passed; 290 call records, 530 total phase records |

Four distinct methods in the red run experienced failed assertions: two direct `TestReport` failures and two methods with two failed child subtests each. Thus 116 of its 120 methods passed without any failing child assertion. The 118 passed `TestReport` records must not be described as 118 completely passing methods. The original red run remains failed. The a3 schema lacks the later top-level `distinct_test_nodeids` and `call_report_types` fields; its 260 passed call records represent 110 methods plus 150 subtest calls.

The exact archived metadata drivers are [a3](evidence/run_2026-10-28/tools/helpers/587e913503a68ca8f54a6c83e08ed7ed40accef92abdd0a940807cebd434571a/check_source_review_metadata_a3.py), [a4 red](evidence/run_2026-10-28/tools/helpers/435a5c4a738498c13dd697877fece4027186fc2fbcece323cba72f6ade7f5fff/check_source_review_metadata_a4_red.py), and [a4 green](evidence/run_2026-10-28/tools/helpers/03ba16042a60fb5173828a6a19f18af2c307e163383378f9a7ac652686992e8b/check_source_review_metadata_a4_green.py). Their directory names are their complete SHA-256 digests. The red-to-green driver diff changes only the recorded `source_state` string and destination JSON filename. Test collection, filtering, report capture, count logic and source-stability assertions are identical. The production consumers changed between those executions, as recorded by their separate source hashes; the test source bytes are unchanged. These were checks of explicitly hashed working-tree deltas before their later commits, not executions falsely relabelled as those commits.
