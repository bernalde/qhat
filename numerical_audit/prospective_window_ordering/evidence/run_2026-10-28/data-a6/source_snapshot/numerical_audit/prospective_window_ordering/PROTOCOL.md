# Prospective normal-window study: preparation contract

Status: pre-data preparation, 2026-10-28. The registry is selected; source/environment/configuration and phase budgets must be frozen before execution. No new chemistry, ordering result or physical error has been measured. Later freeze records name exact committed sources and inputs. This document does not retroactively freeze the earlier proposal notes.

The question is whether opt-in normal Julia conditioned-window insertion improves actual ideal-circuit error versus total construction cost on larger, held-out instances. Four neutral RHF 6-31G/seven-spatial-orbital instances have fourteen modes and four active electrons: two LiH and two BeH2 geometries in REGISTRY.json. They are seen families and new geometry/basis/active-space instances, subject to an authenticated baseline identity scan. Identity failure or chemistry refusal is retained, with no substituted geometry, smaller register, thinned Hamiltonian or complete-panel claim.

## Fixed comparison

All construction uses HF, T=1 and 30U ideal Pauli-word rotations, where U counts ORIGINAL retained words. Eight arms are canonical units, coefficient-magnitude units, retained current placement, retained first-response conserving insertion, retained first-response individual-Pauli insertion, existing full-order sparse cubic training, normal Julia window training with a fresh sparse parent, and normal Julia window training with a fresh magnitude parent. Only supported parent policies are used. Current placement is freshly constructed, charged and used as a declared start for the full sparse control and sparse-parent window arm (along with its reverse and canonical start); both share that initialization policy. The magnitude-parent window arm starts freshly from magnitude without current placement. Exact DP/enumeration is not a production arm.

Two fresh-process repetitions per case retain every result. The second reverses arm order, balancing launch/cache order. Two repetitions are chosen before data to retain the Pauli and first-order controls within a bounded cost; they are not independent chemistry observations or a confidence-interval estimate. Each arm has the SAME absolute64 process-plus-owned-child CPU-second ceiling and320 wall seconds, including imports, authentication, serialization, initialization, parent, Julia startup/JIT/setup, search, full verification and cleanup. The native call receives only the remainder after measured startup/bookkeeping and any declared reserve. Cheap arms keep actual lower costs. No historical parent is free, and no extra64 seconds follow parent construction.

Window policy is width8, one strictly interior window, computed-nonzero-pair selection with earliest tie, at most10 insertion sweeps, checked incumbent retention, and authoritative native full-score acceptance. Original word counts/endpoints determine affordability. The algorithmic/action/entry/storage caps and effective native evaluation/move caps are pinned in the final configuration freeze, before any main outcome. Unsupported/over-cap/deadline runs retain their honest status, unscored initializer or absent result; missing results are censored, never invented zeros.
