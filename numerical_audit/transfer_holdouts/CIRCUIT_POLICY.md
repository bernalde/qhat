This addendum fixes the state-preparation treatment before any issue #17
validation error. The prospective registry, five states, three validation times,
eight arms, search controls, initializers and two rotation caps are unchanged.
The real panel remains gated on the reviewed, frozen issue #16 interface.
Only adapter development and toy correctness checks are currently permitted.

The adapter uses the unchanged `compiled_accuracy/compiler.py` exact-angle
nearest-neighbor line policy inherited by issue #16. Basis changes, restored

## State preparation and circuit scope

HF traces start at the all-zero state and contain the compiler's exact HF
preparation prefix. Their resources cover preparation plus evolution, including
any cancellation at that boundary. Non-HF traces contain evolution only and are
applied directly to the archived ground, excited or coherent state. They never
replace it with HF and never project away leakage. Their quantum preparation
cost is recorded as **unknown**. An evolution-only count cannot establish a
complete correlated-state execution saving.

Classical reference preparation is a separate measured operation. Each unique
training constructor receives its declared reference charge once; complete
warm-start ordering is charged inside cubic construction. Report actual unique
campaign work, shared constructions and standalone charges separately. A shared
HF/state-aware-HF construction or first-order order reused by two formula arms
does not become an independent sample or a second actual ordering cost.

## Affordability and construction

At each 10U or 30U cap, U is the supplied Pauli-term count. First-order formulas
use the largest affordable integer step count. Symmetric formulas fuse adjacent
equal complete units across step boundaries and use the largest affordable
integer step count under that exact rotation count. A one-unit symmetric
formula is represented by its single fused exact unit. Compiler primitive
cancellation can further reduce the trace; it does not change the declared
ideal-rotation cap.

Compilation cost runs from the caller through model authentication and layout
validation, lowering, counting, serialization, hashing, cleanup and return.
Reference preparation, ordering, compilation and physical screening stay
separate. Unlike issue #16's matched-total constructor experiment, this study
uses the already frozen nominal search allowances and reports their complete
costs. It does not claim matched-total construction budgets across arms.

All circuit limits precede allocation: at most six modes, 128 Pauli terms and
units for this inherited compiler/reference path, at most nine supported initial
state entries and 128 formula steps. All six frozen targets fit the inherited
term guard. Existing gate-generation/storage ceilings remain in force. A gate
limit retains a censored partial trace and its actual cost; it cannot receive a
physical error or be described as a completed circuit.

## Verification

Producer replay acts on the complete supplied vector with indexed primitive
actions. An independent extension of the retained literal reference uses sparse
Kronecker matrices, an unfused formula, a separate Taylor reference evolution
and a two-pass circuit dependency DAG. It does not import producer compilation,
fusion, replay or resource-count functions. Toy checks cover real/imaginary
coherent states, both formula orders and orientations, preparation declarations,
actual number leakage, maximal affordability, serialization timing, censored
traces, input/layout substitution and allocation guards.

The circuit adapter and its new checks are initially unexecuted. They must pass
within the existing cumulative 120-CPU-second/900-wall-second unit-check pool
after the running issue #16 worker finishes. Full search/evolution/compilation
preflight and main budgets must be committed before those separate phases run;
the completed chemistry/reference preflights do not stand in for them.

## Complete panel and exact work sharing

`panel.py` fixes all logical identities without searching or propagating. The
main panel retains all 1,440 logical circuits. Exact HF-role and formula sharing
requires 96 outer training constructors and 1,332 unique circuit candidates,
with 90 full-H reference evolutions. A complete cubic constructor still includes
its own fresh first-order warm constructor; it is not replaced by another
arm's measured order. Shared work does not remove logical observations.

Every arm pair within each fixed molecule/state/time/cap setting is retained:
28 pairs per setting and 5,040 main comparisons. Floor labels retain the original
1e-12 error threshold. An absolute difference at or below 2e-12 is labelled a
numerical tie; raw errors and differences remain available. A pair with both
errors at the floor is retained as uninformative; a censored circuit yields an
unavailable pair. Neither case becomes a win or is removed from its denominator.
These rules are fixed before any transfer validation error.

The distinct LiH1.841 full-path preflight uses all five states, eight arms and
both caps at the largest declared time, 2.73. It has 16 outer constructors,
74 unique circuit candidates, 80 logical observations, five reference evolutions
and 280 within-setting comparisons. Its geometry remains excluded from the main
panel. This specifies the representative path, not a claim that it has run or
that its finite execution budgets and feasibility checks are already complete.
